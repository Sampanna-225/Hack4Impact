Current Government Interventions

  • Decentralized Ward Allocation Grid KMC has divided its 32 wards into 7 localized operational blocks, partnering with specialized private utility actors to collect household garbage internally.
  • Source-Level Separation Drives Deploying dual-compartment collection trucks across pilot wards to separate wet biodegradable contents from dry recyclables before routing.
  • Teku Plant Processing Transforming gathered organic kitchen waste directly into premium biological compost at the Teku Transfer Station to feed municipal parks.

A Better Way Forward (Proposals)

  • Strict Fine Execution at Source Imposing immediate municipal fines on households that mix organic and plastic components, moving away from loose compliance guidelines.
  • Decentralized Bio-Gas Infrastructure Building micro-composting fields within each block to process waste locally, reducing the reliance on long-distance hauling to the Bancharedanda landfill.
  • Formalizing Waste Collectors Integrating local informal waste pickers into structural city frameworks with fair wages, health benefits, and proper recycling equipment.

Current Government Interventions

  • Integrated Bus Station Ultimatum Enforcing mandatory routing for long-distance transit through the expanded New Bus Park hub to eliminate unorganized roadside boarding points.
  • No-Parking Zone Designations Clearing inner heritage alleys and main arterials by establishing strict no-parking designations backed by active wheel-locking penalties.
  • Smart Digital Fine Updates Enforcing updated penalties for lane and signal violations under the Motor Vehicle Management guidelines via digital payment portals.

A Better Way Forward (Proposals)

  • Vertical Multi-Storey Public Parking Constructing high-capacity, municipal vertical parking garages near major hubs like New Baneshwor and Khula Manch to clear roadside congestion.
  • Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Corridors Allocating dedicated, physical lanes for high-occupancy electric buses during peak hours to prioritize public transit over private vehicles.
  • Pedestrian-First Commercial Zones Converting crowded commercial streets like Thamel and New Road into fully pedestrianized walk-spaces, restricting all vehicle access during business hours.

Current Government Interventions

  • High-Powered Committee Master Plan Deploying continuous long-term corridor restoration blueprints, completing over 650 consecutive weeks of community-state cleanups.
  • Sewer Interception Corridors Constructing dedicated wastewater diversion pipelines along riverbanks to route raw city sewage away from the river into treatment plants.
  • Guheswori Treatment Expansion Operating structural activation systems at the Guheswori treatment facility to oxygenate water parameters before it runs past historical temple zones.

A Better Way Forward (Proposals)

  • Industrial Effluent Zero-Tolerance Imposing immediate closure penalties on textile facilities and workshops that discharge toxic chemicals directly into the river system.
  • Upstream Eco-Restoration Reservoirs Constructing nature-based rainwater catchment basins in Shivapuri Hills to release clean water during dry seasons, sustaining the river's natural flow.
  • Community Riparian Corridors Replacing concrete riverbanks with natural vegetation buffers and community-managed wetlands to filter urban runoff naturally.
