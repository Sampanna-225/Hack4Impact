Kathmandu Traffic & Parking Compliance Codes

The chaotic yet vibrant streets of Kathmandu are a constant challenge for drivers. Finding a parking spot can feel like a game of chance, and misunderstanding the rules often leads to frustrating and costly fines. But fear not! This essential guide to Kathmandu parking rules is designed to equip you with the knowledge to avoid fines & find spots with confidence. We’ll break down the latest regulations from the Kathmandu Metropolitan City (KMC) and Nepal Traffic Police, ensuring your driving experience in the capital is as smooth as possible.

Why Regulations Matter

Kathmandu experiences significant traffic congestion, making parking a perennial headache. Knowing where to park legally and efficiently protects your wallet from unexpected Kathmandu parking fines and keeps essential thoroughfares unblocked.

Recent System Updates

The KMC has systematically eliminated roadside on-road parking across core commercial nodes like New Road and Dharmapath, actively shifting vehicles toward dedicated off-street multi-story structures.

To effectively find parking in Kathmandu, it’s crucial to understand the designated zones and their corresponding charges. The KMC has primarily divided the city into two zones for parking fee collection:

Enforced No-Parking Zones & Penalties

Ignoring municipal signage can trigger immediate enforcement penalties. Ensure you look out for standard warnings:

  • Common Prohibited Sectors: Areas marked with yellow curbside lines, immediate intersections/turns, pedestrian footpaths/sidewalks, and near ambulance terminal entrance systems.
  • Violation Penalties: Fines for parking violations range from Rs. 500 to Rs. 1,500. Vehicles blocking critical traffic lines are subject to immediate mechanical wheel clamping or flatbed towing by the municipal towing division.
  • Document Compliance Audits: Traffic patrols actively verify registration validity during parking checks. Operating without a valid license or registration can result in immediate vehicle impoundment.

Digital Fine Settlement

Long queues at police counters have been phased out. Follow these steps to resolve an issued digital citation via smartphone:

  1. Citation Issuance: Officers register violations digitally against the QR-code index of your license (synchronized through the national Nagarik App platform).
  2. Review Details: Access the Nagarik App interface, enter the Driving License tab, and locate the pending record under the Violation Records registry to find your custom voucher code number.
  3. Digital Remittance: Launch an integrated digital mobile wallet such as eSewa or Khalti, input the specific voucher alphanumeric string, confirm the transaction, and store your payment confirmation receipt.
  4. Offline Resolution Alternative: Remittances remain payable at physical branch tellers of Rastriya Banijya Bank Ltd. Ensure you collect the stamped transaction voucher to verify compliance.
